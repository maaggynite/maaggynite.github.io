Sample project

Employee attrition analysis (Tableau)

Objective
Identify attrition patterns and workforce factors linked to higher turnover.
Approach
Analyzed attrition KPIs across departments, roles, tenure, overtime and job satisfaction. Built two interactive dashboards and an executive report.
Data
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition and Performance dataset (public).
  • Overtime employees had a 30.53% attrition rate versus 10.44% for those without overtime.
  • Low job satisfaction was associated with a 22.84% attrition rate.
  • Sales had the highest departmental attrition at 20.63%.
  • Employees with under one year of tenure had the highest attrition of any tenure group.
  • HR priorities: manage overtime, strengthen first-year onboarding, review Sales Representative roles.
